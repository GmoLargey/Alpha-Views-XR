Free, open-source software (GNU GPL version 3).
See your real hands, steering wheel and flight controls inside PC VR games with
added depth support, using your headset's passthrough camera.

NEW IN THIS VERSION
- Lock button (padlock, above the window, left end): locks the window's angle.
  Moving it then never tilts or turns it. Gold while locked. Saved per window.
- Snap button (pale blue, above the window, right end): squares the window up
  with the game world, facing you. The first press stands a tilted window back
  upright; each further press switches between upright facing forward, upright
  facing the side, and lying level.
- Boxes can now be as deep as you like (up to 100 m, was 1 m). The thumbstick
  speeds up on deep boxes.
- The green box handle and the keep-box button now sit on the middle line of
  the box, a little further out, so they stay put on deep boxes and keep clear
  of the corner handles on short windows.
- Shrinking a window with a corner only deletes it when you shrink it below
  2 cm, so short windows no longer vanish when you touch a corner.
- Unreal Engine 4 games (e.g. Hubris): windows now stay put in the world
  instead of following your head.
- Earlier: circle windows, free shapes, keep boxes, key colours, presets and
  more (see README.txt).

BEFORE YOU START
- Windows 10/11 (64-bit), and a headset with passthrough streaming PC VR.
- Check the right OpenXR runtime is selected (the one for your streamer).
  SteamVR-Settings.png in the install folder shows where to check it.
- Passthrough: if your streamer or PC runtime has an alpha channel
  (transparency), turn it on. If not, use its colour-key passthrough and pick
  the same colour here (Ctrl+Alt+C).
- Games must run in OpenXR mode with Direct3D 11 (Direct3D 12 is experimental,
  Ctrl+Alt+D, see README). SteamVR-only games don't work, because SteamVR
  removes the transparency.
- No eye tracking? Touch the handles directly with your hand or controller.

AFTER INSTALLING
Try the test scene first (Start menu: "Alpha Views XR test scene").
Gestures.png, Controllers.png, Keyboard.png and Shapes.png in the install folder
show all the controls. Full guide and troubleshooting: README.txt.

A zip version (no installer) is also available separately.
